The saying that you need to eat rice for your brain to work well? I now think it's half right and half wrong. Have you ever had a clearer head on the day of an important presentation or deadline when you skipped lunch? I only understood why after trying the carnivore diet myself. When I cut out carbohydrates, I felt **brain fog** actually lift, in my body, before I understood it in my head.

## Why do we get sleepy after eating? Ketones were the answer

On days I needed to focus, I used to skip lunch out of habit. Whenever I ate meat with rice, without fail my energy would slowly drain away and my eyelids would grow heavy. At first I thought it was simply a willpower problem, but only after starting the carnivore diet did I realize it had nothing to do with willpower.

The cause was the sugar crash. A sugar crash is when your blood sugar shoots up after eating carbohydrates and then drops quickly, bringing on intense fatigue and drowsiness. It happens repeatedly when the brain depends on glucose, an unstable source of energy.

When I switched to meals centered on meat, butter, eggs, and cheese, that roller coaster disappeared. I stayed refreshed after meals, and my focus didn't waver all afternoon. Research confirms this isn't just a feeling. When you restrict carbohydrates, the liver produces a **ketone body** called beta-hydroxybutyrate (β-hydroxybutyrate). Ketone bodies are produced when fat is broken down, and they are an alternative fuel that supplies neurons with steady energy even when the brain is insulin resistant. According to a study published in *Frontiers in Psychology* (2019), ketone bodies were reported to reduce inflammation in neurons and genuinely improve cognitive function ([Source: Frontiers in Psychology](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology)).

<strong>In short:</strong> the root cause of post-meal drowsiness and brain fog is blood sugar swings, and switching your energy source to ketones gives the brain a stable fuel that keeps your mind clear.

## How many days until the carnivore diet works?

When I first started the carnivore diet, honestly, this surprised me: around day three, my head felt even foggier and my body felt heavy. That stage is the **keto flu**: temporary fatigue, dizziness, and poor concentration that appear while the body adapts to using fat instead of glucose for energy. When you first experience it, it's easy to give up, wondering "Is this right?", but once you get over this hump, things change.

In my experience, I felt the effects between days 7 and 14. As blood sugar swings settled, the foggy state after meals began to disappear, and I could feel my thinking stay clear through the afternoon work hours. After 30 days, this state stabilized. This is when gut inflammation decreases, the brain's networks use **ketone** energy steadily, and overall cognitive function rises.

The numbers confirm this isn't just my experience. In a study involving the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health, more than 91% of 2,029 people who had followed a carnivore diet for six months or more reported improvements in memory and mental clarity ([Source: Current Developments in Nutrition, 2021](https://academic.oup.com/cdn/article/5/12/nzab133/6415894)). Mental clarity refers to a state in which thinking is sharp, reactions are quick, and focus holds without distracting thoughts.

Here is what the adaptation stages look like:

- **Days 1–7 (adaptation):** temporary fatigue and dizziness from the keto flu. Plenty of fluids and electrolytes are key.
- **Days 7–14 (early effects):** blood sugar swings disappear; post-meal drowsiness and brain fog begin to ease.
- **Day 30 and beyond (the shift):** gut inflammation decreases, ketone energy stabilizes, and mental clarity and focus improve in earnest.

<strong>In short:</strong> the carnivore diet's effect on brain fog takes at least two weeks, and more than 30 days to feel it fully; the key is getting past the early keto flu.

## Salt cuts sugar cravings: an unexpected effect of the carnivore diet

On a carnivore diet, salt intake naturally goes up. At first, that actually worried me, given the common belief that too much sodium is bad for your health. But when I tried it, I found that when I got enough salt, my craving for sweets dropped sharply. There's evidence that this isn't just my imagination.

Cardiovascular researcher Dr. James DiNicolantonio explains that when the body lacks sodium, it runs the **renin-angiotensin system** abnormally. The renin-angiotensin system is a hormonal system that regulates blood pressure and the balance of water and electrolytes; when it is overactivated, the body becomes less efficient at turning fat and protein into energy, creating a kind of "internal starvation." In response, the brain sends **dopamine** signals to seek out the fastest source of energy: simple sugar.

Conversely, when you get enough salt, this abnormal hormonal signal switches off and the instinctive craving for sugar decreases. In my experience, when a sudden craving for sweets hit, drinking a little sea salt or pink salt dissolved in water calmed the craving within minutes. I think this mechanism is why the carnivore diet doesn't stop at cutting carbohydrates but leads to healthier eating habits overall.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I improve brain fog without going carnivore?

Even without cutting carbohydrates completely, simply reducing refined carbohydrates and processed foods can ease blood sugar swings and improve brain fog. In my experience, though, the speed and strength of the effect were proportional to how much I restricted carbohydrates. The more strictly you restrict them, as with keto or carnivore, the faster the switch to **ketones** and the more clearly you feel mental clarity.

### The keto flu is really hard. How do I get through it?

The main cause of the keto flu is a lack of electrolytes. When you cut carbohydrates, the kidneys excrete sodium faster, so actively replenishing salt, magnesium, and potassium is the key to getting past it. During this stage, drinking water with salt dissolved in it helped me quite a bit. The hardest part usually passes in three to five days.

### Is eating a lot of salt on a carnivore diet bad for blood pressure?

It's generally believed that sodium raises blood pressure, but when carbohydrates are restricted, insulin release drops and the way the kidneys reabsorb sodium changes. For this reason, people on carnivore or keto diets often actually need more salt. That said, if you have an underlying condition, be sure to consult a medical professional before starting.

### How can I tell whether my brain fog is a diet problem or a sleep problem?

If fogginess keeps coming back after every meal even when you've slept enough, it's worth suspecting blood sugar swings first. I had a pattern where my focus dropped sharply around 2 or 3 p.m. after lunch even when I'd slept well, and that pattern disappeared after I cut out carbohydrates. If improving sleep alone doesn't solve it, I think checking your diet is an efficient approach.

## Conclusion

Brain fog is not a lack of willpower. For a long time I simply put up with the fog that came every afternoon, but it turned out the cause was the blood sugar swings created by carbohydrates. When I switched my energy source to **ketones** with a carnivore or keto diet, the fog disappeared, and now I deliberately choose this way of eating on days I need to focus.

The first two weeks bring the keto flu, but the change in cognitive function you feel after keeping it up to 30 days is unmistakable. If you have an important project, exam, or presentation ahead, it may be worth trying to restrict carbohydrates for at least two weeks. However, if you have an underlying condition, be sure to consult a medical professional first.
